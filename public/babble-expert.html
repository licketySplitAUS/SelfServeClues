My screen looks different / I’m stuck

These realistic screens are AI recreations based on Windows and Mac examples, with Babble Expert’s name and icon. Colours, wording and button positions may vary.

Windows: if there is no “Run anyway”, Smart App Control, Windows S mode or your organisation’s policy may be blocking installation. Don’t turn off security protection. Ask someone you trust to help, or contact lickety.

Mac: try opening Babble Expert from Applications first, then return to Privacy & Security. Older Macs call it System Preferences → Security & Privacy → General. If “Open Anyway” is missing, or the message says the app is damaged or will harm your computer, stop and contact lickety.

Email licketySplitAUS@gmail.com. Include whether you use Windows or Mac and the exact message you see. Never send your password.
